# astrologer.ai
## Executive Summary

Version: MVP v1.0

---

# Vision

Build India's most premium AI-powered personal guidance platform.

The first product is AI Palm Reading.

Users upload a palm image.

AI analyzes the major palm lines and generates a personalized report.

The first goal is not to build an astrology platform.

The first goal is to build a product that converts cold Meta traffic into paying customers.

---

# Product Philosophy

People are not buying astrology.

People are buying curiosity.

The landing page should create curiosity.

The report should create delight.

The premium reports should create trust.
